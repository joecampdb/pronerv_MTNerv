What if a molecular energy landscape were made from the molecule itself?

This exploratory prototype starts with 750,000 public MD frames of alanine dipeptide. Molecular conformations form translucent pathway skins that deform through an atom-attached displacement field. A simplicial nerve can be superimposed to make two different kinds of composition visible:

• inclusion of overlapping conformational neighborhoods;
• concatenation of observed transition-route types.

The distinction matters. A categorical composite A → B → C is not automatically an observed direct A → C transition. And composing paths is only a first step toward composing dynamical models with explicit interfaces.

The accompanying illustrated note explains the construction, its LaTeX mathematics, and its limits. The figures were rendered in Blender from the same geometry as the interactive viewer.

This is an experiment in scientific representation, not a new validated kinetic model. The deformation is driven by MD data but is a visual interpolation, not additional simulated dynamics.

I would welcome perspectives from applied category theory, molecular simulation, and structural bioinformatics: what should the interfaces be if we want molecular dynamical models to compose?

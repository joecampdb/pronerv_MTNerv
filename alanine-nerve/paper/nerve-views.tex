\section*{Measured embedding of the overlap nerves}
These revised plates use the full-data witnessed covers and supported mobility
heights. Each vertex is an object of $P(K_W^{(2)})$: a witnessed index set.
Circles, squares and triangles distinguish sets of size one, two and three.
Edges are strict inclusions; a plate is a chain
$\sigma\subset\tau\subset\upsilon$, including the composite inclusion.
Edge direction is suppressed; inclusion points from smaller to larger sets.
Bars show conditional resampling spread, not calibrated confidence intervals.

\begin{figure}[H]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/alanine-measured-nerve.png}
\caption{\textbf{Alanine: the revised measured-height nerve in two views.}
The category contains 78 objects, 212 inclusions and 138 triangular chains.
The embedding includes 76 objects with supported heights. Missing-height
objects and seam-crossing geometry remain in the categorical metadata but
are omitted from this flat drawing. The interactive torus view respects
angular identifications. Vertical position is structural mobility at 100 ps,
in nm; it is not empirical free energy. Example labels refer to the revised
node array.}
\end{figure}
The full underlying complex is preserved by maximal simplices, with maximal
dimension three. These plates depict the face-poset nerve of its 2-skeleton;
they do not claim to display every higher-dimensional simplex. Linear
triangles may intersect in the embedding without creating new incidences.

\section*{Measured embedding of the CLN025 overlap nerve}
\begin{figure}[H]\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/chignolin-measured-nerve.png}
\caption{\textbf{CLN025: revised full-deposit nerve in two views.}
All 131 display objects have supported heights; the category has 466
inclusions and 360 triangular chains. Horizontal axes are C$\alpha$ radius
of gyration and end-to-end distance. Height is the equal mean of
root-conditional structural mobilities at ten frames, in nm; entire inferred
initial-conformation cohorts are resampled together. The clock is unresolved.
Marker and edge conventions match the alanine plate.}
\end{figure}
The full witnessed base complex has maximal dimension five. Its higher
simplices are preserved in metadata and included through tetrahedra in
the $H_2$ calculation, even though only a finite categorical substructure
is drawn. Neither the elongated shape nor the measured height establishes
a folding coordinate or a configuration-space homotopy equivalence.
These edges are overlap inclusions, not kinetic transitions.

\textbf{Reproduction.}
Run \texttt{python build\_validated\_atlas.py}, then
\texttt{python render\_revision\_figures.py} from \texttt{alanine-nerve/}.
The scripts use numerical witnesses, supported heights and exported chains;
no connectivity is inferred from pixels. Earlier monochrome occupancy-height
plates remain historical assets rather than being overwritten.
